**Index page**
    In the Index page, also known as homepage, I decided to use section tag with <h2> and <p> for the purpose of prompting user to be directed
to given pages. However, while validating HTML, I was return an error massage as " "button" a bad value for attribute "role" on element "section" in HTML" (See AI coversation line:313 to line:333). This was caused by not fully understading the function of "role" attribute. With the AI's explaination, I gained the knowledge that the "role" attribute serves nothing but marking down the purpose of a tag. For instance, in my case, althought "button" is an avaliable role value, a section tag should not function as a button in general meaning. AI suggested me using "article" instead, yet, I found out that navigation fits the most in my case.
    Another usage of AI was at the begining phase, I was suggeseted using buttons to direct users to different pages. However, due to the blankness of this page, buttons does
not look nice wuth brower size changing. Hence, I choose not using what AI prompted me (see AI conversation line:267 to line:311). However, I used JavaScript AI given to me with a little modification. Such as not using const, as it got me overwhalmed learning it at this phase. Instead, I adopted "window.location.href = "../html/about.html";" with EventListener inspired from other JavaScript, I made my idea worked.

**Footer**
    I decided to add validation certification on the footer. I was in between of creating a seperate page contains validation image or just a image itself. I asked AI giving me
advice. I turns out that <a href="img_url"> tag solves my question better as if I were to create new pages I might have to create 9 pages, which was less efficient. Eventually, I raised another question " how am I supposed to go back to last page?". I then asked AI how (see AI conversation line:494 to lin3:560), as I felt clicking return button on browser is less challenging. It suggested me adding a "target" attribute with "_blank" value:
<a href="your-image-url.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
    <img src="thumbnail-image.jpg" alt="Description of Image">
    View Image in New Tab
</a>

    I found out that rel attribute and <img> tag is unnecessary in my case. So I sticked with adding target too all footer <a> tags.
